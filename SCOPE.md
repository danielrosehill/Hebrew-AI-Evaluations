# Scope

In: evaluations I (Daniel Rosehill) designed or ran that measure AI on modern Hebrew, including Hebrew inside English
(code-switching), grouped by task (text-to-speech, speech recognition, image generation, OCR, LLMs, translation...).
Each entry links its repo, data and results.

Out: benchmarks, evaluation sets and leaderboards by others (they belong in
[Hebrew-AI-Benchmarks](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Benchmarks)); evaluations that do not involve Hebrew
(they are in [Experiments-And-Evaluations-Index](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Experiments-And-Evaluations-Index)).
